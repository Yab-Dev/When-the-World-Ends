## About
When the World Ends is a real-time strategy game where you are fighting against an overwhelmingly powerful AI. A doomsday timer constantly ticks down to zero signifying the point-of-no-return for the AI's strength. You are placed in command of all the world's troops in order to buy as much time as possible. Take out key AI targets, move your troops strategically, and make difficult decisions in order to make every second count.

## Controls
Click on the title of a window to move it, click on a black dot to open the continent menu, and click on an orange dot to open the battle menu.
